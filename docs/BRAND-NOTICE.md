# AP brand asset

`public/brand/ap-logo.svg` is the AP Thailand logo used in the application header. It is served locally at its original aspect ratio without recoloring or redrawing.

Verified on 6 October 2026 against the inline `.i-icon:logo-ap` SVG on the [official AP Thailand homepage](https://www.apthai.com/th). The [user-provided Living Pulse reference](https://ap-living-pulse-poc.ap-thailand-5069.chatgpt.site/brand/ap-logo.svg) supplies the identical 644-byte SVG (viewBox `0 0 24 38`, SHA-256 `ee9e4dfe1e5ad5f50ccf413de3d167c863dda3338eeb7d5ce4e1067e8194c176`).

The AP name and logo remain the property of their owner and are not relicensed under this project's software license. Replace the logo with an authorized brand asset when adapting this application.

The deployed dashboard uses its existing AP fonts. The public demo retains IBM Plex Sans Thai under the included OFL license; proprietary AP fonts and real project exports are not copied into the public repository.
